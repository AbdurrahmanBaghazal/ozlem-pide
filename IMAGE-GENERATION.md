# Generated Menu Imagery

The 37 images in `public/menu-images/` were created with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project and optimized to 1000 × 1000 WebP. They are **illustrative**, not photographs of food prepared by Raykent Özlem Pide. `menu-image-map.json` records which image is assigned to each of the 64 menu products. Similar products share an image where an exact item image was not generated.

The menu labels the imagery as representative. The admin can replace a product image with a restaurant-supplied photograph at any time. Actual photos should be approved by the restaurant before public launch.

## Prompt set

The images used this shared direction:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: square restaurant digital-menu product photo. Style: photorealistic editorial Turkish food photography, credible ingredients, on a simple ceramic plate or glass over a warm terracotta tabletop, natural restaurant light, centered three-quarter overhead view, clean square crop. No people, no logo, no text, no watermark, no extra dishes.

Each image then specified one of these subjects: minced-meat pide; cheese pide; mixed pide; diced-beef pide; sucuk-and-egg pide; cheese-and-egg pide; lahmacun; cantık; Adana kebab; Beyti kebab; grilled köfte; grilled chicken; mixed grill; lamb şiş; beef saç; chicken saç; kiremitte köfte; çoban salad; mixed cold drinks; künefe; eggplant kebab; Ali Nazik; grilled chicken wings; liver şiş; yogurt köfte; çiğ köfte; haydari; cola; ayran; water; şalgam; orange soda; fruit juice; pizza; roasted onion salad; şıra; and lemon soda.
